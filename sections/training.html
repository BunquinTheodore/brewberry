How a session flows

Five moves to your first pour.

  1. 1 Espresso

    Step 1. Dial in and dose

    Weigh, grind, adjust. Feel how a finer grind or a slightly heavier dose changes what lands in the cup.

  2. 2 Technique

    Step 2. Tamp with intention

    Level, firm, consistent. The quiet little press that decides whether your shot runs sweet or sour.

  3. 3 Milk

    Step 3. Steam silky milk

    Stretch, then spin. Chase that glossy, wet paint texture that makes latte art possible.

  4. 4 Latte art

    Step 4. Pour and practice

    Pour, wipe, repeat. Hearts first, then whatever your hands are ready for. The best part, you get to drink your homework.

  5. 5 Certificate

    Step 5. Graduate with a certificate

    Wrap up with a certificate of completion and a photo at the bar. Framing it is optional. Bragging is encouraged.
