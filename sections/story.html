Brewbery Kitchenette

How it began

Two crafts, one little kitchen.

It started with two people and one shared counter. A barista who could talk about crema for hours, and a pâtissier who never leaves the oven until the croissants turn golden. Together they opened a small kitchenette where coffee and pastry are made side by side, so every cup comes with the perfect bite.
